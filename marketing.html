An AI analyst that checks itself

Ask "how is Solana doing?", "compare Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana" or "what is fully diluted value?". Answers are built from figures the app computes; Meta's Llama 3.2 runs on your device to phrase them. Anything it writes that contains a number, a name or a reason that isn't in the data is discarded, and the app's own wording is shown instead.

It never predicts prices and never tells you to buy, sell or hold — questions asking for either are declined, with the facts it can give. And it only works while you are watching: leave the screen or ask something new, and the previous answer stops.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your watchlist, holdings and alerts are stored only on your device, and the AI runs on it too. Show every figure in dollars, euros, pounds, rupees, dirhams, riyals and more — or in bitcoin.

What it won't do

It won't predict prices, rate coins, set targets or recommend trades. It won't connect to a wallet or an exchange, and it won't run in the background. It shows what the market is doing, clearly — the decisions stay yours.

Crypto assets are highly volatile and you can lose all of the money you put in. This app provides market information only.
